## Top reasons ReviewPilot could fail (and what to watch)

1. **"Nice to have" trap:** owners like it but won't pay €69. _Signal:_ trial→paid < 15% after onboarding improvements. _Response:_ push harder on groups, and cut Essentials if support costs exceed its revenue.
2. **Commoditisation by Google:** native AI replies make the core free. _Signal:_ churn reasons citing "Google does this now". _Response:_ the Issues/intelligence wedge must be proven by month 12.
3. **Distribution failure:** a great product with no cheap channel. _Signal:_ CAC > €800 per location sustained. _Response:_ partnership-first, or pivot to selling via POS vendors as an embedded white-label.
4. **Platform dependency:** one Google decision can halt the business. _Response:_ diversify sources over time. Keep the relationship with Google clean (strict policy compliance).
5. **Building too much before validation:** _Response:_ design partners in month 5–6, and kill features with low usage.
