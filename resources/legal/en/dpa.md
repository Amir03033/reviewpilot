## 1. Subject

The processor processes personal data only to provide the ReviewPilot service, on the customer's documented instructions. The settings the customer chooses in ReviewPilot count as instructions.

## 2. Data and data subjects

|                        |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

| **Data subjects**      | Reviewers on Google; the customer's users and team members                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Data**               | Reviewer display name as shown on Google, review text and rating, date, replies; names and email addresses of users                                                                                                               |
| **Special categories** | Not intended. Reviews may contain health information, especially for healthcare practices. It is not processed separately, sensitive reviews are flagged for human handling, and healthcare practices never get automatic replies |
| **Operations**         | Fetching, storing, AI analysis, drafting, publishing, reporting                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Duration**           | Term of the agreement plus the deletion period in section 8                                                                                                                                                                       |

## 3. Processor obligations

The processor:

- processes data only on the customer's instructions, unless the law requires otherwise;
- ensures people with access are bound by confidentiality;
- takes appropriate technical and organisational measures (section 5);
- assists the customer with data subject requests, DPIAs and regulator enquiries;
- does not use the data for its own purposes or to train AI models.

## 5. Security

HTTPS for all connections; encrypted storage of Google tokens; logical separation of data per organisation; role-based access; two-factor authentication; audit log of all publications; daily backups; least-privilege staff access; timely security updates.

## 6. Data breaches

The processor notifies the customer of a personal data breach without undue delay and at the latest within 48 hours of discovery, with the information the customer needs to notify the supervisory authority.

## 7. Transfers outside the EEA

Transfers only take place to the listed sub-processors, based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## 8. End of the agreement

After cancellation or deletion of the organisation the customer can export its data. The processor deletes the data no later than {{disconnect_grace_days}} days afterwards, except data that must be kept by law (such as invoices). Backups are overwritten in the normal rotation.

## 9. Audit

On request, the processor provides the information needed to demonstrate compliance. An on-site audit is possible once a year, at the customer's expense and with 30 days' notice.
